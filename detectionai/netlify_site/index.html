Face Scanner

A private companion app that scans faces through the recognition service running on your own PC.

Download Android APK

Your face photos and profile details stay on your PC. This website does not collect or process them.

How it works

  1. Run the Face Scanner server on your PC.
  2. Connect your phone and PC to the same Wi-Fi network.
  3. Open the Android app, allow camera access, then tap Connect.
  4. Tap Scan Face to match against profiles stored locally on the PC.

Start the PC scanner

cd E:\website\face_scanner
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe run.py

For desktop camera use, open http://127.0.0.1:5000 on that same PC.

Phone connection

Enter the current PC Wi-Fi address in the app, for example http://192.168.29.163:5000/. If the PC joins another Wi-Fi network later, run ipconfig on the PC and replace the address with its new Wi-Fi IPv4 address.

Privacy note

This hosted page is only a download and setup guide. The face recognition service is intentionally kept on your local network rather than deployed to the public internet.
